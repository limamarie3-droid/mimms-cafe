Frequently asked questions

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, it's free for customers. Network: MIMMsCafe-Guest. Ask at the counter for today's password.

Outlets are along the window wall. Please buy something every couple of hours so others can find a seat.

What about allergens?

Our cheesecakes contain dairy and eggs. Crusts contain tree nuts. Lattes can be made with oat, soy or dairy milk. We work in a shared kitchen, so we can't promise anything is free from traces of nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs or soy.

If you manage a health condition, please check with your doctor. Tell us about any allergy when you order and we'll show you the ingredients.

Can I order from my phone?

Yes. Call or email us with your order and pickup time, and we'll have it ready at the counter. Pay when you pick up. We confirm every mobile order by reply, so if you don't hear back, your order isn't placed. Whole cheesecakes need 48 hours' notice.

What is your refund policy?

If your order is wrong, cold, or not what you asked for, tell us on the same day. We'll remake it or refund it to your original payment method. Refunds usually show up within 5 to 10 business days.

Whole-cheesecake orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before pickup. Use the order problem form or call us.

Track your order

Enter the order number from your receipt or confirmation. We update order statuses by hand during opening hours. Sample order numbers to try: 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004.
